Senior UX Designer · Warsaw, Poland

I design complex things
for people who know
their domain deeply.

For the past 9 years I've specialised in UX for internal tools, enterprise platforms, and data-intensive workflows — the kind of products where the users are experts, the data is dense, and getting the interaction model wrong has real consequences.

My work sits at the intersection of discovery and delivery. I run stakeholder interviews and user research to understand what's actually happening — not just what's been specced — map complex processes, and translate them into interfaces that reduce cognitive load and make hard decisions easier.

Experience

Recent work includes 3.5 years embedded in a global aerospace client at Accenture — designing internal engineering tools including a prediction monitoring interface and a financial calculation system used across engineering teams.

Before that: Egnyte (B2B cloud platform), Allegro (internal admin tooling), and two Polish banks.

How I work
  • 01 Discovery first — stakeholder interviews and user research before wireframes, always.
  • 02 Decisions based on data, not opinions. I prototype competing solutions and test them.
  • 03 Full cycle ownership — from ambiguous brief to developer handoff and beyond.
  • 04 Comfortable with high stakes. I've designed for million-dollar financial calculations and safety-critical engineering tools.
  • 05 Fully remote, international clients, C1 English.
